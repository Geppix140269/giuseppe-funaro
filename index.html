Profile

International commercial leadership built over three decades

I am an international commercial leader with more than 30 years of experience building businesses, opening markets and developing revenue across Europe, the UK and the US.

My career has combined hands-on business development with senior commercial and general-management responsibility. I have held consolidated P&L responsibility exceeding €200M, scaled a European division from €70M to €220M, negotiated $30M+ long-term commercial agreements and led teams across multiple international markets.

I have worked across telecommunications, infrastructure, energy, international procurement, industrial supply and digital trade intelligence.

More recently, I have been exploring a simple question: what becomes possible when decades of commercial experience are combined with AI as a practical tool? The result has been a series of working products and market experiments across sport, international trade, property and wellbeing.

I am particularly interested in opportunities where international commercial experience, AI-enabled execution and entrepreneurial thinking can create growth: business development, international expansion, strategic partnerships, market creation and new ventures.

I am based in Barcelona, native Italian and fluent in English and Spanish, with extensive international mobility.
